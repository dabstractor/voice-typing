- `RealtimeSTT/audio_recorder.py:291–303,485–488` and `RealtimeSTT/core/realtime.py:529–549,577–645`: the plain `on_realtime_transcription_update(str)` reports the latest formatted hypothesis (not a delta). `on_realtime_transcription_stabilized(str)` reports formatted `display_text` (stable prefix **plus unstable preview**, or raw text fallback). Both may revise or retract text. Neither is a final. Preview strings trim/collapse whitespace and may capitalize, but do not append the final sentence period (`core/text_formatting.py:18–44`, `core/realtime_text_stabilizer.py:460–520`).
- The comment in `voice_typing/daemon.py` suggests plain could be earlier; **1.0.2 tries both legacy callbacks in the same eligible decode, stabilized first inline**. They share active-recording, nonblank, and initial-delay checks; stabilized is not gated on `stable_delta`, `accepted`, or `should_publish`. The stabilizer still runs when registering plain only. The difference may be useful visible content and revisions rather than time of first invocation. Keep the separate structured callback and any new recognition engine out of scope unless evidence warrants a separate design. `text()` final is a separate path (`core/transcription_api.py:22–44,111–136`).
- `start_callback_in_new_thread=False` is the constructor default (`audio_recorder.py:184`, `core/initialization.py:166–174,609–626`, `core/state.py:14–21`, `core/realtime_callbacks.py:8–23`). The callback normally runs inline on the realtime worker; per-dispatch threads are not an ordered fast path. Select exactly **one** callback for delivery before construction/load; no runtime swapping, double-delivery, waiting on the worker, or moving CUDA to the daemon. Keep the stream-edit callback unchanged. Actual selected source and device/cadence must be in the G5 provenance.
- [Python multiprocessing spawn](https://docs.python.org/3.12/library/multiprocessing.html#contexts-and-start-methods): fresh interpreter; monkeypatching a parent-only constant is not an A/B experiment. Carry a private, picklable selector over the existing feed-only recorder build path and through both initial/CPU-fallback construction paths. Production selection is internal to the output mode; no public config switch needed. [Python Condition](https://docs.python.org/3.12/library/threading.html#condition-objects): predicate recheck under lock, bounded waits in tests; notification alone does not establish state. [pytest monkeypatch](https://docs.pytest.org/en/9.0.x/how-to/monkeypatch.html): patch the actual lookup, scope changes per test, and complete/join workers before mocks unwind. Do not globally patch all `subprocess.run` calls to hide cross-file media I/O.

Only isolated, same-clip replays can establish which callback contains the next useful word soonest, incurs fewer actual corrections/dumps, and meets per-route visibility measures. ASR-ready, daemon-ready, target ACK and field/frame time are distinct; upstream docs alone cannot establish a G5 PASS.
